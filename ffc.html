Our Story

A Corner of Freetown's Heart

Born in the bustling streets of Aberdeen and nurtured by generations of family recipes, Freetown Food Corner is more than a restaurant — it’s a celebration of Sierra Leone’s rich culinary heritage.

We source the freshest produce from local markets, the finest fish from the Atlantic, and cook with the same patience and love that has defined Freetown kitchens for decades. Every plate tells a story of community, tradition, and the vibrant spirit of our city.
